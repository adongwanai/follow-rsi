## 1.1 Search & evolution (frozen model) · 1

- **EmoHarness**：EmoHarness uses a Builder to revise prompts, settings and restricted verification code from a frozen student model’s failure traces. A separate validation split decides whether a candidate replaces the current Harness for later rounds. Most gains come from audio evidence, and the incremental evolution gains do not survive multiple-comparison correction. Human developers inspected some evaluation items, so the evaluation is not an untouched holdout.
