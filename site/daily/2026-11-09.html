CPU-ARC

CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse

CPU-ARC 将推理步骤实现为可复用的 Python 语义函数。构建器依据执行错误、judge 反馈和检查结果反复修改候选，通过后注册到系统级缓存，供后续推理调用。纳入依据是反馈驱动的可执行组件更新；论文还包含小模型微调实验，不能将整篇概括为权重完全冻结，也不能只凭推理节省断言计入构建成本后的净收益。

CPU-ARC turns reasoning steps into reusable Python semantic functions. Its builder revises candidates using execution errors, judge feedback, and checks, then registers successful functions in a system-level cache for later inference. The qualifying mechanism is feedback-driven revision of executable components. Separate small-model finetuning experiments mean the whole paper should not be described as weight-frozen, and inference savings alone do not establish net savings after construction costs.
