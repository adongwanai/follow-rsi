MIRIX-2.0

MIRIX-2.0: Multi-Agent Memory System

MIRIX 的 AutoDream 程序性技能分支根据会话轨迹判断成败，再由 curator 创建、编辑或删除供后续 Agent 使用的技能指令。训练任务形成的技能库冻结后，在新任务上评估复用。纳入依据是反馈驱动的程序性步骤修订，语义记录合并与事实检索不属于这条证据；实验评估的是整套流程，未单独证明每次技能修改或某个组件的收益。

The procedural-skill branch of MIRIX AutoDream judges session outcomes from execution transcripts, then uses a curator to create, edit, or delete skill instructions for later agents. Skills acquired on training tasks are frozen and evaluated for reuse on new tasks. Inclusion rests on feedback-driven revision of procedural steps, separate from semantic-record consolidation and factual retrieval. The experiments evaluate the combined process, without isolating the benefit of every skill edit or individual component.

MetaAct: Self-Evolving Executable Action Constraints for Embodied Language Agents

Continual-ARC

Continual-ARC: Measuring Skill Acquisition over a Working Lifetime

Continual-ARC 用反复出现且实例更新的 ARC 任务流，比较可保留程序和技能文件的 Agent Harness 与清空状态的对照；流结束后冻结系统，分别测试已见任务的新实例和未见任务。论文观察到，持久化本身的记忆收益有限，明确的程序编写、验证与复用指导能改善已见任务表现，而未见任务的泛化收益仍很小或没有。纳入为 Harness 专项评测；反馈预算可比不代表模型训练与教师成本完全匹配。

Continual-ARC evaluates persistent agent harnesses on a stream of recurring ARC tasks with fresh instances, using reset controls and frozen seen-task and held-out tests. The tested systems retain programs and skill files across sessions. Persistence alone generally gives limited memorization gains; explicit guidance to write, verify and reuse programs helps on returning tasks, while gains on unseen tasks remain small or absent. This is a harness-focused evaluation, not evidence that feedback-budget comparisons match training and teacher costs.
