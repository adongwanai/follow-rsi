## 4. Model–harness co-evolution · 3

- **HarnessAdapt-RL**：During RL, one teacher generates task-related mechanism-level skills, while another evolves tools and hooks from recurring interaction problems; retains only edits that improve pass rate or trainability without lowering the other metric. [hf](<https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28069>) [arxiv](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28069>) [code](<https://github.com/lingxiaotang/HarnessAdapt-RL>) [card](../../papers/harnessadapt-rl.md) — 4 other checks await verification（Curated evidence; see evidence card）
  Original publication: 2026-10-20 · HF recommendations: 2026-10-21
- **CoEvo-RSI**：A meta agent uses a shared evolutionary archive for bidirectional inheritance of historical harnesses and model checkpoints, fine-tuning on its own rejection-sampled trajectories to mitigate “brain–body mismatch.” [hf](<https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.25078>) [arxiv](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25078>) [code](<https://github.com/SuJQ0722/CoEvo-RSI>) [card](../../papers/coevo-rsi.md) — 4 other checks await verification（Curated evidence; see evidence card）
  Original publication: 2026-10-18 · HF recommendations: 2026-10-21
