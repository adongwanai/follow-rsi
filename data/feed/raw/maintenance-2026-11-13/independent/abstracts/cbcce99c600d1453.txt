E-commerce LLMs are increasingly important for product understanding, recommendation, search matching, substitute identification, and product question answering. However, deploying these capabilities in one system remains difficult because e-commerce tasks have heterogeneous formats, noisy and long-tailed product text, structured output requirements, tight inference budgets, and uncertain teacher-supervision quality. Existing studies often rely on large closed models or separate task-specific pipelines, which limits low-cost unified deployment and leaves unclear how teacher-generated data affects compact student models. We present UniCommerce, a 1.5B-parameter unified e-commerce LLM built on Qwen2.5-1.5B. UniCommerce reformulates six ECInstruct tasks—PM, PRP, SR, MPC, PSI, and AG—into a schema-aligned generation framework with structured prompts, CoT-guided teacher annotation, answer-span masking during SFT, template-guided output formatting, and unified parsing and evaluation. It achieves strong task-level performance across the evaluated systems, including 0.9868 F1 on PM, 0.7788 F1 on PSI, 0.7855 accuracy on MPC, 0.5483 HR@1 on SR, and $0.9143~\mathrm {F}_{\text {BERT}}$ on AG, while maintaining an average runtime of 2.143 seconds and a whole-suite cost of ${\$}4.42$ . Ablation results show that schema guidance, teacher supervision, user-side context, and answer-span masking jointly support performance. Teacher-sensitivity analysis further shows that teacher-annotation quality depends on schema consistency and learnability rather than teacher strength alone, providing a new perspective on low-cost, unified, and teacher-aware e-commerce LLM deployment.
