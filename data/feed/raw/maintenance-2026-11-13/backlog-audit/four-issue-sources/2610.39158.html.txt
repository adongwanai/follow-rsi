VEvo-Harness: Causally-Guarded Self-Evolvi ng Harness for Agentic Image Generation Yuan Wang Affiliation: Taobao & Tmall group of Alibaba Affiliation: Tsinghua University Yuhang Yu Affiliation: Taobao & Tmall group of Alibaba Mengting Chen Affiliation: Taobao & Tmall group of Alibaba Affiliation: Corresponding Author Tianrun Xu Affiliation: Tsinghua University Zihao Pan Affiliation: Taobao & Tmall group of Alibaba Xuetao Feng Affiliation: Tsinghua University Wei Li Affiliation: Taobao & Tmall group of Alibaba Abstract The central bottleneck in visual generation and editing has migrated from achieving pixel-level fidelity to satisfying densely constrained user specifications through increasingly agentic workflows. Prevailing agentic generation pipelines remain shallow generate-verify-rewrite loops treating VLM judgments as coarse visual feedback, either discarding repair experience or admitting ungated updates that poison skill library. With memorize and govern periods left open, they degrade into ungoverned iterators of repeated trial-and-error rather than continual self-evolving harness. Towards this issue, we present VEvo-Harness , the first causally-guarded continual self-evolving harness system for agentic visual generation. VEvo-Harness introduces four coupled innovations: (1) a visual traceback that densifies sparse judgments into root-cause attributions with a hierarchical verification tree; (2) a visual continual governance that admits only causally valid skill patches through counterfactual dual-arm image generation replay; (3) a lifecycle-managed harness state that hierarchically distills root-cause-anchored episodes across working, experience, and pattern tiers into a persistent skill library of routable and causally verified capabilities; (4) a heterogeneous sandbox policy that orchestrates a diverse action space of repair primitives, dynamically routing cost-aware interventions based on root causes to select the most effective strategy. VEvo-Harness surpasses state-of-the-art agentic methods and rivals proprietary generators ( e.g. , GPT-Image, Nano-Pro) while maintaining the continual harness evolution and generalization. 1 Introduction Recently, multimodal generation ( Song et al., 2026 ; Hu et al., 2026 ; MiniMax-AI, 2026 ; Seedance et al., 2026 ; Chen et al., 2026b ; Liu et al., 2026 ; Chen et al., 2026c ; Chen et al., 2024 ) has evolved from research capability into general-purpose utility. Leading proprietary systems such as GPT-Image ( OpenAI, 2026 ) and Nano Banana ( Pichai, 2024 ) , alongside prominent open-source frameworks like Qwen-Image ( Wu et al., 2025 ) and Z-Image ( Cai et al., 2025 ) achieve unprecedented levels of fidelity. As these models become ubiquitous creative tools, research attention shifts toward increasingly complex prompts. Modern generation requests steadily outgrow a single inference pass, as a growing fraction hinge on exact object counts ( Kamath et al., 2025 ) , attribute binding ( Zhou et al., 2026 ) , external knowledge ( Pan et al., 2026a ; Wang et al., 2026b ) , or multi-step reasoning ( Wang et al., 2026c ) beyond aesthetic description. The central bottleneck has thus shifted from pixel-level fidelity to satisfying multi-faceted specifications under densely coupled constraints. To counter this escalating complexity, recent work shifts scaling from model weights to test-time computation, i.e. , prompt densification ( Wang et al., 2024 ; Yang et al., 2026 ) and semantic rewriting ( Zhan et al., 2024 ; Yang et al., 2024 ) . However, prompt-only interventions lack fine-grained attribution and pixel-grounded feedback, while newer multi-agent systems ( Feng et al., 2026 ; Chen et al., 2026d ; Chen et al., 2026a ; He et al., 2026b ) surround generator with planning, web search, skill invocation, and refinement loops. Despite this progress, their harnesses function as read-only control-flow orchestrators. GEMS and Gen-Searcher augment generation via iterative verification or external retrieval, yet their stateless or statically curated harnesses discard repair trajectories and freeze capability maps at deployment. GenRouter evolves only bucket-level routing utilities while leaving the routed repair loops and their admission criteria untouched, so none of them possesses a governed write path through which verified experience become persistent capability. Moreover, sparse visual feedback makes such naive correlation-based accumulation self-deceiving, inducing harness-level hallucination. We thus ask how the scaffolding of a frozen visual generator can consolidate cross-task repair experience into persistent state under a causally governed write path, without collapsing into such hallucination ? The emerging agent harness ( Kang et al., 2026 ; Karten et al., 2026b ) treats the scaffold around models as a site of continual adaptation to accumulate capability. Extending this to visual synthesis, however, demands an explicit causal guarantee. In Fig. 1 , we present VEvo-Harness , to our knowledge the first continual causally-guarded self-evolving harness for agentic image generation with four principles: (1) Visual traceback. Unlike coding harnesses ( DeepSeek-AI, 2026 ; OpenClaw, 2026 ) with line-level tracebacks, visual critics provide only coarse binary feedback ( e.g. , pass/fail), because visual failures are continuous and multi-scaled. We construct a hierarchical verification tree that dynamically expands failed judgments into fine-grained failure modes ( e.g. , attribute binding), yielding structured root-cause signals that anchor experience memory and causally verified skill updates. (2) Visual continual governance . To prevent harness hallucination, we introduce a visual continual evaluator that estimates each candidate patch’s causal effect via counterfactual dual-arm replay under pinned skill routing, and admits only patches with positive estimated effects, thereby keeping the skill library causally validated. (3) A lifecycle-managed harness state. We organize persistent memory into working, instance, and pattern tiers, distilling root-cause-anchored repair episodes into reusable failure patterns that are causally verified before promotion into routable skills. The lifecycle enables the harness to accumulate causally verified experience and self-evolve monotonically across tasks. (4) A heterogeneous sandbox policy. Rather than hard-wiring the harness to a single prompt-rewriting tool, we expand its action registry into a heterogeneous sandbox, and route failures with a cost-aware policy based on visual traceback and persistent harness state. Our approach contributes threefold: • We propose VEvo-Harness , the first causally-guarded self-evolving harness for agentic image generation. It recasts the scaffold around a frozen generator into a persistent, causally verified state space, establishing a new paradigm of harness-level capability accumulation. • VEvo-Harness integrates four innovations, i.e. , counterfactual dual-arm replay for visual continual governance, hierarchical verification for precise failure attribution, lifecycle-managed state for experience accumulation, and sandbox routing policy for adaptive intervention. • Extensive experiments across holistic generation and editing benchmarks show that VEvo-Harness continuously self-evolves via accumulated experience, yielding reset-free monotonic capability growth and zero-shot generalization to highly complex visual instructions. 2 Related Works Agentic Image Generation. Recent work reframes image generation from text-to-pixel decoding as an agentic runtime, replacing prompt expansion and heuristic rewriting with planning, retrieval, verification, and iterative repair. Qwen-Image-Agent ( Zhang et al., 2026b ) casts generation as context construction, while Mind-Brush ( He et al., 2026a ) , Gen-Searcher ( Feng et al., 2026 ) , and RS-Gen ( Bian et al., 2026 ) use search and reasoning to ground knowledge-intensive prompts. CRAFT ( Kovalev et al., 2025 ) and SCOPE ( Ren et al., 2026 ) move toward structured control by maintaining explicit visual constraints and invoking targeted repair only when violations arise. GenClaw ( Ye et al., 2026 ) adds executable visual code as an intermediate canvas for layout control, whereas AR-RAG ( Qi et al., 2026 ) performs autoregressive patch-level retrieval during decoding. GEMS ( He et al., 2026b ) and GenRouter ( Chen et al., 2026a ) extend this toward agent-native runtimes with persistent memory and adaptive workflow routing. They remain tied to fixed workflow families, leaving fine-grained visual attribution and harness-level governance of heterogeneous visual failures insufficiently addressed. Our VEvo-Harness, a unified visual generation scaffold, fill this gap via causally guarded self-evolution. Agentic Harnesses and Scaffolding. Recent work treats harness as runtime infrastructure rather than prompt wrapper. Ning et al. (2026) identify tool orchestration, state management, and execution verification as core harness functions, and Karten et al. (2026a) emphasizes long-horizon performance depends on persistent substrates, i.e. , continual memory, durable state, reusable skills, and runbook control. Lou et al. (2026) ; Zhang et al. (2026b) show substantial gains come from synthesizing, revising, or optimizing harness itself through environment feedback, regression-tested edits, or interface-side adaptation. Thangarajah et al. Further extensions cover behavior localization, lifecycle governance, runtime mutation, and structured policy-environment interfaces Wang et al. (2026a) ; Garralda-Barrio (2026) ; Hou et al. (2026) ; Gan et al. (2026) . Prior work focuses on coding, search, and web agents, leaving visual generation underexplored, where sparse feedback and stochastic execution demand causal tracing and persistent adaptation. Framework Overview. As illustrated in Fig. 2 (a) and Algo.1, VEvo-Harness forms a bidirectional closed loop between a frozen generation model M M and an evolving harness ℋ t \mathcal{H}_{t} , which integrates external tools ( e.g. , web search, code execution) to drive iterative generation via targeted interventions. In the forward flow, a user prompt is first parsed into verifiable visual constraints. The routing policy π \pi then queries the lifecycle-managed harness state (Sec. 3.3 ) ℳ \mathcal{M} to retrieve failure-aware episodic traces from ℰ \mathcal{E} and applicable heuristics from 𝒮 \mathcal{S} , which guide workflow construction and prompt enhancement. In the backward flow, failures of generated images are localized by visual traceback (Sec. 3.2 ) and addressed via the heterogeneous sandbox policy (Sec. 3.5 ). The repaired trajectories are assessed by visual continual governance (Sec. 3.4 ), which promotes only causally validated gains into the persistent skill library and retains the others as episodic experience. With generate–verify–intervene–memorize loop, the harness continually evolves through recursive self-improvement. 3.2 Visual Traceback Agentic visual generation relies on a judge VLM to evaluate a set of compositional visual constraints c c —spanning object counting, attribute binding, and spatial relations—against a synthesized image y cand y_{\text{cand}} , yielding a sparse Boolean verdict vector 𝐯 ∈ { 0 , 1 } m \mathbf{v}\!\in\!\{0,1\}^{m} , where v j = VLM ​ ( y cand , c j ) v_{j}\!=\!\text{VLM}(y_{\text{cand}},c_{j}) . Crucially, these constraints are cached per prompt to ensure strict criterion alignment across all arms of the downstream counterfactual protocol. While sufficient for accept/reject decisions, binary signals obscure visual failure attributions ( e.g. , spatial distortion or attribute misbinding), offering no actionable, pixel-aligned guidance for targeted intervention when v j = 0 v_{j}\!=\!0 . As shown in Fig. 2 (b), we introduce the Visual Traceback mechanism, which densifies these non-differentiable global verdicts into structured, semantically grounded root causes, enabling precise diagnostic feedback for downstream repair. Visual traceback densifies each failed boolean verdict into localized root causes via a per-constraint two-level verification tree 𝒯 j \mathcal{T}_{j} , whose root encodes the coarse requirement c j c_{j} and expands lazily only when v j = 0 v_{j}\!=\!0 . Upon failure, the VLM is prompted to generate a bounded set of finer-grained sub-questions that disentangle the failure mechanism. This generation is explicitly guided to span orthogonal diagnostic dimensions—encompassing object existence, fine-grained attributes, and stylistic fidelity—while semantically redundant sub-questions are dynamically filtered. Unlike a predefined dimension checklist, this failure-conditioned adaptive decomposition tailors the diagnostic structure to the specific semantic nature of c j c_{j} ( e.g. , relational constraints expand into spatial and existence checks). All sub-questions of all failed constraints are evaluated concurrently, bounding the diagnostic overhead to a single parallelized VLM round. Tab. 1 shows that VEvo-Harness achieves the best average performance on both backends, reaching 76.28% on Z-Image-Turbo ( Cai et al., 2025 ) and 77.59% on Qwen-Image-2512 ( Wu et al., 2025 ) . Gains are strongest on compositionally demanding benchmarks: on GenEval2, VEvo-Harness reaches 86.1% with Qwen-Image-2512, outperforming GenRouter ( Chen et al., 2026a ) by 14.5%, while Promptist ( Hao et al., 2023 ) degrades by 19.3% on DPG-Bench ( Hu et al., 2024 ) under Z-Image-Turbo. VEvo-Harness also leads on OneIG ( Chang et al., 2026 ) (0.594/0.572) and WISE ( Niu et al., 2025 ) (0.88/0.86), indicating root-cause decomposition, causal skill verification, and sandbox routing improve both compositional fidelity, retrieval-augmented synthesis, and knowledge-intensive reasoning. By distilling failure/success experiences into verified patterns, VEvo-Harness enables reliable knowledge reuse that improves cross-task generations. With the scaffolding of VEvo-Harness, Qwen-Image surpasses commercial Seedream-4 ( Seedream, 2025 ) on GenEval (0.90), releasing more scaling potential than single-loop and routing-only optimization. Performance on Downstream Tasks. The downstream tasks in Tab. 2 verify that capability growth resides purely in agent harness, with VEvo-Harness improving average by 17.21% and 11.58% over the original backends. Inference-time scaling methods, e.g. , Maestro improve certain metrics but degrade others, revealing that unguided search or experience accumulation triggers performance trade-offs across diverse constraints. VEvo-Harness avoids this by closing generate-verify-intervene-memorize loop around a persistent state space. It distills domain-specific preventive patterns for text rendering, spatial reasoning, and aesthetic fidelity from root-cause-anchored episodes, while counterfactual dual-arm replay further gates every patch before deployment keeping skill library causally validated. VEvo-Harness achieves state-of-the-art results, elevating CREA by 13.89% and 10.23%, and ArtiMuse by 17.15% and 4.81% on two backends. These gains show that accumulated harness state transfers zero-shot to specialized domains, confirming reset-free growth at harness layer. Performance on Image Editing Benchmarks. Tab. 3(a) identifies lifecycle-managed state as the main driver of self-evolution, as removing persistent memory drops GenEval2/KRIS by 6.7%/6.2% and disabling skill consolidation still costs 2.5%/3.3%, confirming that retained experience compounds only after distillation into reusable skills. Counterfactual governance beats Admit-All by 2.9%/3.2% and exceeds the fixed checklist of Text-Only Gate by 2.2%/2.7% on all benchmarks, validating image-based verification before skill updates. HSP improves over Rewrite-Only by 4.0% on WISE and over random routing by 2.4% on KRIS, while progressive assembly raises the marginal GenEval2 gain from 1.8% to 2.7%, showing the complementary value of action diversity and adaptive routing. Tab. 3(b) further shows that removing root-cause-anchored instance memory causes the largest degradation (-3.96%) with latency rising to 45.2s, counterfactual reranking contributes 1.80%, and EMA updates plus variance regularization add 2.62% and 1.97%. On cost, lazy gating cuts VLM calls 47.5% at a 0.28% gain, the denied-change buffer blocks redundant replay that would inflate calls 51.6%, and removing success preservation costs 2.75% and 43.8s at unchanged calls. The full setting is thus the most accurate (84.38%), fastest (38.4s), and cheapest (6.2 calls) variant. Self-evolution via Governed Knowledge Accumulation. Figs. 4 (a) and (c) link skill-update quality to sustained gains, as VEvo-Harness lifts its rolling-average GenEval2 score from 63.2 to 86.5 by distilling recurring failures into reusable skills. Without visual continual governance (VCG), gains collapse after a mid-stream peak near 78 to about 71.5, ending roughly 15% below the full model, since unverified updates pollute the skill library. Fig. 4 (a) attributes this collapse to admission quality, where No Gate admits 33–40% harmful patches and Text-only Gate settles near 16%, whereas full VCG reaches about 2% by 100 episodes, rising to 10% and 8% without the denied buffer or success preservation. GEMS and the stateless variant finish near 70 and 64.5, confirming that cross-task reuse requires persistent state. Thus lifelong memory and causally-guarded governance are complementary, with persistent state enabling reuse and counterfactual replay filtering updates before propagation. Governance Efficiency and Redundancy Suppression. Across 300 streamed episodes, the denied-change buffer progressively reduces verification overhead and harmful admission rates by filtering previously rejected edit directions (Fig. 4 (b)). Without it, repeatedly evaluating structurally similar but previously rejected interventions inflates VLM calls by 71.8% over the task stream. With the buffer, the call count instead drops by 25.7%, yielding a 5.1% reduction in the first interval that widens to 59.0% by episodes 241–300 and 41.6% on average (10.82 to 6.32). Fig. 4 (a) and the prolonged repair trajectories in Tab. 3(b) show that removing success preservation degrades the admission curve: without shielding correctly rendered elements, the agent repeatedly breaks and re-fixes the same regions. Caching rejected patches further reduces redundant verification and lowers harmful admission rate. Causal Matching of Heterogeneous Interventions. Fig. 4 (d) reports P ⁡ ( Δ c ​ f > 0 ) P(\Delta_{cf}\!>\!0) for each sandbox action conditioned on failure type, exposing a strongly heterogeneous intervention–response surface. Conditioning-side failures favor prompt rewriting policy, with rewrite reaching 0.66 on compositional misbinding, whereas renderer-side defects invert this preference, as the layout blur responds more reliably to hyperparameter tuning (0.57) than to prompt rewriting (0.29). The sub-dim deficit failures do not admit a single dominant action and therefore require stochastic routing over action set. Matched actions outperform mismatched ones by up to 8.7x , indicating that monolithic pipelines structurally bottleneck when a fixed intervention is applied across heterogeneous failure modes, while routing π ⁡ ( a | 𝐞 ) \pi(a|\mathbf{e}) over traceback root causes recovers per-type intervention specificity without exhaustive replay. Fig. 5 evaluates the transferability of VEvo-Harness across heterogeneous foundation backbones under identical configuration. VEvo-Harness consistently improves both lightweight and frontier models. On GenEval2, Qwen3-VL-8B increases from 74.6% without the VCG to 82.6% with full harness, while Kimi-K3 ( Team et al., 2026 ) improves from 84.0% to 90.1%. The larger GenEval2 gains further show that root-cause diagnosis and intervention routing transfer effectively across models, supporting VEvo as a robust visual-generation scaffold. 5 Conclusion We introduce VEvo-Harness , a reset-free, causally guarded framework elevating the agent scaffold from a transient execution layer to a persistent, self-evolving runtime for agentic image generation. Visual Traceback employs a hierarchical verification tree to decompose visual judgments into localized root-cause vectors for fine-grained diagnosis. Visual Continual Governance uses counterfactual dual-arm replay to admit only causally validated patches while preserving verified solutions. Lifecycle-Managed Harness State structures memory into working, experience, and pattern tiers alongside a patch-evolvable skill library, distilling episodic experiences into reusable capabilities. Heterogeneous Sandbox Policy establishes a generalized action routing that dynamically aligns diagnosed root causes with diverse interventions via risk-aware utility estimation. Extensive results across general generation benchmarks confirm that VEvo-Harness achieves substantial gains over agentic pipelines. These results chart a path toward self-evolving agents in e-commerce AIGC workflows ( e.g. , visual creative generation and virtual try-on), accumulating user-specific abilities through continuous interactions.
