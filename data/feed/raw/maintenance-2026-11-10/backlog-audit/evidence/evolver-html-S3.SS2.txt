3.2 Experience Representation and Asset Management Structured Strategy Representation. To preserve the knowledge acquired through historical exploration, Evolver represents reusable procedural knowledge as structured strategy assets rather than retaining complete execution trajectories as the primary unit of reuse.
Building on the strategy-gene representation [ 9 ] , each Gene g i g_{i} is characterized by g i = ( s i , 𝒮 i , 𝒫 i , ℳ i , 𝒱 i ) , g_{i}=(s_{i},\mathcal{S}_{i},\mathcal{P}_{i},\mathcal{M}_{i},\mathcal{V}_{i}), (2) where s i s_{i} summarizes the experience, 𝒮 i \mathcal{S}_{i} specifies procedural steps, 𝒫 i \mathcal{P}_{i} describes applicability conditions, ℳ i \mathcal{M}_{i} provides task-matching signals, and 𝒱 i \mathcal{V}_{i} contains optional validation plans.
This representation separates reusable operational guidance from task-specific execution details, allowing historical strategies to be retrieved and adapted to subsequent tasks.
Importantly, applicability conditions and validation plans provide guidance rather than enforceable execution constraints or guarantees of prior verification. Complementary Experience Assets. Beyond Gene objects, Evolver maintains complementary assets that preserve execution context and support experience management.
A Capsule associates a strategy with a concrete execution case, providing contextual examples that can accompany retrieved strategies.
An AntiGene captures negative procedural guidance, identifying actions or practices to avoid in subsequent executions.
Meanwhile, Event records capture asset-related operations, while separate provenance and review records track experience origins, trust status, and approval decisions.
Together, these components constitute the persistent experience state ℰ t \mathcal{E}_{t} , separating reusable strategies, supporting execution cases, and operational metadata. Persistent Asset Management. Evolver maintains experience assets through content-addressed, append-only storage, preserving asset identity independently of subsequent lifecycle decisions.
Review and provenance states are maintained in separate records, supporting experience quarantine, approval, rejection, and revocation without overwriting the underlying assets.
This separation enables persistent procedural knowledge to coexist with evolving eligibility and trust states, providing a foundation for feedback-driven experience management.
The enforcement of these states depends on the corresponding acquisition and retrieval pathways, as detailed in Sections 3.3 , 3.4 and 3.5 .
