Appendix F Data Coverage and Prospective Controlled Comparisons Training and evaluation separation. The nominal training pool contains 2,400 semantic-verifier tasks and 600 supplementary tasks. SWR100 evaluation tasks do not overlap with this training pool. Reproduction additionally requires category frequencies, task and repository manifests, workflow-variant mappings, and sampling weights so that exposure and broader source reuse can be inspected. Controlled tests should preserve protected verifiers, match task exposure and training conditions, and measure strict success independently of shaped reward. Structural-proxy definitions and aggregation. The fixed strata in Figure 10 are constructed from 468 benchmark–task pairs across six suites. Checker scale is the larger of the extracted checker count and the number of distinct static test functions. File footprint counts data, tests, environment files, and reference artifacts rather than files repaired by the agent. Instruction length is measured in characters rather than tokens. These proxies measure structural complexity rather than a universal scale of cognitive difficulty. Missing structural fields are assigned zero, which can underestimate incompletely documented tasks. The frozen cutoffs are approximately 0.37, 0.51, and 0.69 for display; assignments use the full-precision thresholds preserved in the structured difficulty-stratification records and place ties in the lower stratum. The four-suite subset contains 322 benchmark–task pairs: 118 Easy, 106 Medium, 57 Hard, and 41 Extreme. Scores average full-pass indicators over each task’s regular repeats and then weight tasks equally. Existing attempts without a reward count as failures in this analysis. The Terminal-Bench 2.1 RSR-1 analysis substitutes an alternative retained evaluation report for the originally designated evaluation, which is unavailable. The RPR-2 stratum scores use the corresponding retained evaluation report; its checkpoint identity has not been independently reconciled with the retained step-18 Terminal-RSI-27B checkpoint. These provenance constraints and task-weighted aggregation distinguish the stratified analysis from the primary suite-level comparison.
