Training execution and in-training monitoring. The per-GPU training-token budget is 8,192, log-probability calculations use chunks of 1,024 tokens, and RPR-2 uses microbatch size one with padding to multiples of 2,048 tokens. The 8,192-token response limit applies to one generation rather than the cumulative multi-turn trajectory. SGLang uses a static memory fraction of 0.75 and a chunked prefill size of 8,192 tokens. The saved RPR-2 configuration uses FP32 gradient accumulation, FP32 attention softmax, and zero dropout. Appendix F describes the 2,400-plus-600 task mixture and its separation from SWR100. The preparatory protocol trains the critic for one SWR epoch with the actor frozen and retains the resulting ϕ seed \phi_{\rm seed} . RPR starts from the RSR-1 actor and freezes each phase’s actor during four initial critic-only updates before joint training (Section 2.4 ). The saved RPR-2 launch configuration requests weights-only transfer without restoring optimizer or random-number-generator states. Available records do not resolve every actor–critic weight pairing or isolate warmup’s contribution to value calibration and sample efficiency. Reproducing the preparatory epoch requires verifying full task coverage, actor freezing, and successful critic export before RPR-1; weight continuity alone does not imply identical optimization dynamics.
