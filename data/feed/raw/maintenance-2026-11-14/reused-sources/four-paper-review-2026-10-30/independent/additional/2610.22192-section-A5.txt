Our final benchmark protocol reports the arithmetic mean of three independent evaluation runs (Appendix A ). Historical summaries contain heterogeneous repeat counts; stating the final protocol does not retroactively supply missing runs or independently recompute their aggregates. Neither a historical two-run label nor a three-response training-monitor configuration establishes the repeat count of every historical entry. In particular, 5/46 does not assert that each run solved the same five tasks. We retain the reported entries without inferring per-run task identities or rounding an unobserved mean. Historical task coverage. The nominal historical denominators are 89, 70, 63, and 100 tasks for Terminal-Bench 2.1, 3.0, 4.0, and Hard, respectively. Earlier records instead use 74 Terminal-Bench 3.0 tasks and 66 Terminal-Bench 4.0 tasks. Task manifests, run-level results, and infrastructure-failure handling must therefore be reconciled before assuming identical coverage or independently verifying every aggregate against the final protocol.
