monitoring with 11 semantic assertions and a Rust–C expression evaluator with two terminal gates. Separate
from benchmark Mean@3, 11 drift trials yield 1, 3, 2, 2, and 2 scored repeats, excluding one unscored Base
trial; ten evaluator trials yield two per stage. Scores average checker completion, not best-run or binary task
success. RSR-2 and Terminal-RSI-27B have separate event records and verifier outputs, both reaching
100.00% checker completion on both tasks (Appendix H).
