2.2 Reward evolution: family calibration and success prioritization
FDR calibrates partial progress; SPR separates strict success from bounded partial credit.

task family. Both rewards use valid Common Test Report Format (CTRF) grading or fall back to S.

quantile of total non-hygiene calibration checks N, with fallback 82:
