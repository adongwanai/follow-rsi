4  Related Work Recursive supervision. STaR, ReST, and ReST-EM establish generate–filter–train approaches, while Reflexion improves behavior through verbal feedback [ 51 , 10 , 39 , 37 , 42 ] . RSR rewrites cross-harness discoveries; its runbook reviewer is not a learned value critic [ 23 ] . T1 combines terminal PPO, token fidelity, and warm-started values [ 46 ] . Terminal-RSI couples these components through critic-preserving transitions. Policy optimization for long-horizon agents. Building on clipped surrogate objectives, critic-free methods estimate advantages via group, leave-one-out, or batch normalization with tailored token aggregation [ 35 , 36 , 48 , 1 , 14 ] . Other variants stabilize updates through length-normalized sequence ratios, second-moment token masking, or mismatch-adaptive clipping, whereas Terminal-RSI adopts fixed asymmetric clipping [ 53 , 54 , 47 ] . Meanwhile, critic-based approaches leverage learned value baselines, initialization, and skip-observation credit assignment [ 50 , 49 , 13 ] . Alternative formulations regress log-ratios toward optimal advantages, approximate critic-free mirror descent, center prefix-conditional scores, or learn from pairwise preferences rather than verifier outcomes [ 32 , 40 , 26 , 52 ] . Long-interaction and asynchronous training systems. Recent agentic training pipelines support interaction-chunk optimization with persistent runtime states and couple synthesized tasks with mismatch-controlled asynchronous updates, enabling direct policy optimization on capable base models without supervised fine-tuning [ 33 , 16 ] . Concurrently, distributed reinforcement learning architectures decouple actor trajectory generation from learner policy updates across varying compute scales to maximize system throughput [ 5 , 30 , 8 ] . Furthermore, scalable post-training infrastructures accelerate large-scale reinforcement learning by bridging distributed model-parallel training backends with high-throughput structured rollout execution engines [ 56 , 38 , 55 ] . Tasks, environments, and the object of recursion. TerminalTraj supplies executable-verified trajectories, while CLI-Gym constructs repair tasks by environment inversion [ 45 , 25 ] . Terminal-Task-Gen combines adapted and skill-synthesized data; SkillSynth samples scenario–skill graphs; CLI-Universe grounds fail-to-pass tasks in technical documentation [ 31 , 6 , 15 ] . RST recursively expands aligned solutions, environments, and verifiers and studies multistage supervised and policy optimization [ 19 ] . Environment Evolution schedules scenario and skill complexity; Terminal-Universe reconstructs environments from trajectories [ 7 , 44 ] . These construction curricula complement our critic handoff and reward calibration. SWR and LHTB separate progress resolution from feedback frequency [ 20 , 22 , 21 ] rather than implying matched baseline performance.
