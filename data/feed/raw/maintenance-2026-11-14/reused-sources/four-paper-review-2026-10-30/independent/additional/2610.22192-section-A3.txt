An action-independent baseline preserves the expected unclipped on-policy Monte Carlo policy gradient when the sampling policy is the differentiated policy and differentiation can be interchanged with integration; this does not guarantee an unbiased numerical advantage estimate or extend unchanged to clipped asynchronous PPO [ 43 , 9 ] . Consistency conditions. Unit clocks with matched values, rewards, and episode endpoints recover standard GAE at retained generated positions. Adding zero-reward observation nodes leaves TB-GAE unchanged when retained prefix inputs and value predictions are fixed. Computational partitioning preserves the result when both successor value and successor trace are transported without changing retained inputs. Context summarization changes retained conditioning and is therefore outside this exact fixed-input invariance. Reward sums alone are insufficient: with unit clocks, turn rewards 0.10 and 1.00 imply returns 1.10 and 1.00, whereas moving their sum to termination incorrectly gives 1.10 to the second turn. Computational boundaries must preserve episode identity, turn structure, and causal reward placement rather than automatically terminate the episode. These invariances establish estimator properties rather than evidence of deployment in a particular evaluated checkpoint.
