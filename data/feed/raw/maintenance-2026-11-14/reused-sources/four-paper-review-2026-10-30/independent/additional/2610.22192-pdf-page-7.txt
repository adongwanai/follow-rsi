Benchmarks.
We evaluate Terminal-Bench 2.1, 3.0, 4.0, and Hard, together with LHTB and SWR100
[19, 20, 22, 27, 28, 41]. LHTB filters 120 candidate projects to 46 tasks with reference solutions and hidden
tests; broken projects require multi-turn repair across long dependency chains, with weighted subtask grading
and hidden stress cases. We report mean process reward and strict completion at score 1.00, not the 0.95
near-completion threshold. The source SWR benchmark derives six variants from each of 500 executable
workflows spanning 46 families and six domains; this construction is distinct from our 2,400-plus-600 training
mixture. SWR100 tests 100 reconstructions from public input–output examples against hidden semantic
configurations, structural checks, and anti-shortcut checks.
Evaluation protocol. Each retained stage checkpoint is evaluated on each benchmark in three independent
runs, reporting their arithmetic mean (Mean@3), not pass@3 or a union of solved tasks. LHTB counts use
the compact notation mean strictly completed tasks/46. Sampling uses temperature 0.60 and top-p = 0.95,
at most 8,192 output tokens per turn, and 96,000 input tokens per call. Up to 100,000 turns are permitted,
subject to benchmark termination and time limits. Proactive summarization triggers at 32,000 accumulated
tokens, compressing prior context before execution continues; it is not a terminal event. Following standard
evaluation practices for large-scale RL, multiple independent evaluation runs rigorously capture stochastic
inference variance and ensure result reliability. Appendices H and E document the separate qualitative and
historical PPO protocols.
