3.5 From local repairs to complete verification Task-level studies reveal the execution contracts behind aggregate scores. We examine embedding-drift monitoring with 11 semantic assertions and a Rust–C expression evaluator with two terminal gates. Separate from benchmark Mean@3, 11 drift trials yield 1, 3, 2, 2, and 2 scored repeats, excluding one unscored Base trial; ten evaluator trials yield two per stage. Scores average checker completion, not best-run or binary task success. RSR-2 and Terminal-RSI-27B have separate event records and verifier outputs, both reaching 100.00% checker completion on both tasks (Appendix H ). Output equivalence exposes a verifier-coverage gap. The Rust–C evaluator stays at 50.00% through RSR-1: the wrapper passes but exact-output fuzz testing fails.
