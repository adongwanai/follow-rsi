F Data Coverage and Prospective Controlled Comparisons
Training and evaluation separation. The nominal training pool contains 2,400 semantic-verifier tasks
and 600 supplementary tasks. SWR100 evaluation tasks do not overlap with this training pool. Reproduction
additionally requires category frequencies, task and repository manifests, workflow-variant mappings, and
sampling weights so that exposure and broader source reuse can be inspected. Controlled tests should preserve
protected verifiers, match task exposure and training conditions, and measure strict success independently of
shaped reward.
Structural-proxy definitions and aggregation. The fixed strata in Figure 10 are constructed from 468
benchmark–task pairs across six suites. Checker scale is the larger of the extracted checker count and the
number of distinct static test functions. File footprint counts data, tests, environment files, and reference
artifacts rather than files repaired by the agent. Instruction length is measured in characters rather than
tokens. These proxies measure structural complexity rather than a universal scale of cognitive difficulty.
Missing structural fields are assigned zero, which can underestimate incompletely documented tasks.
