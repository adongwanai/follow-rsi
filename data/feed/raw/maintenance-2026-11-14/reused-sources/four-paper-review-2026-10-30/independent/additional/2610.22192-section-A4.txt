For positive target mass, p → 0 p\to 0 penalizes loss of represented families, but the bound supplies no guarantee of discovering absent families or restoring entropy. Native replay must preserve the task and protected verifier; removing privileged hints does not make unsupported actions publicly justified. Prospective checkpoint selection requires predefined validation objectives, patience, tolerances, evaluation frequency, and retention criteria. The reported sequence records retained checkpoints rather than validating a prospective selection rule. Repeated selection using final benchmark feedback would confound learning with test-set adaptation. Conservative policy-improvement arguments likewise distinguish surrogate gain from distribution-shift error [ 18 ] . Five stages of one 27B backbone establish neither a model-size scaling law nor an equal-compute benefit of recursion.
