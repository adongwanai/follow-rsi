In this proof-of-concept, Apollo 2 autonomously performs automotive kitting: the repetitive, physically taxing task of sorting individual parts from bulk bins into organized trays in the exact order needed for each vehicle on the line. Offloading this monotonous prep work helps keep the assembly line moving, while freeing up technicians to focus on complex build tasks.
