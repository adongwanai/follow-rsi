**Summary**: Harness population selection: a “preserve and expand” contract accepts only variants that do not regress on evaluated tasks; an archive retains multiple lineages and merges complementary expert harnesses. Non-regression is checked only on a finite evaluation set.

**Key result**: TB2.1 75.5%→83.2%; TerminalWorld held-out set 68.3%; WebArena-Infinity 43.5%→93.0%; transfers to SWE-bench Verified without modification.
