**Summary**: A supervisor–worker structure: the supervisor corrects or stops the worker in real time during execution, while distilling the procedures and failure patterns exposed in that run into persistent skills and memory.

**Key result**: Up to 9.8pp above the corresponding harness on TB2.0; in the self-improvement setting, GLM-5.1 +14.6 and Kimi-K2.6 +12.4.
