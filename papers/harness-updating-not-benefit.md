**一句话**：「产出好更新」的能力与模型强弱几乎无关（Qwen3.5-9B 接近 Claude Opus 4.6）；「从更新中获益」则非单调，中等模型获益最大。

**Summary**: The ability to produce good updates is almost unrelated to model strength (Qwen3.5-9B is close to Claude Opus 4.6); the ability to benefit from updates is nonmonotonic, with medium-sized models benefiting most.
