**Summary**: Multiple evolutionary islands maintain lineage trees and historical memory, retaining failed mutations as negative evidence; an orchestrator adjusts lineage grafting, differentiation, editing-agent allocation, and curriculum.

**Key result**: With Opus 4.8, TB2.1, PaperBench, and DeepSWE gain +12.0%, +28.3%, and +10.3% over the initial harness, versus +4.5%, +18.3%, and 0% for the previous best search method.
