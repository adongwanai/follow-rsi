**一句话**：同一个冻结模型在三个层级工作：任务 Harness、改写它的演化器、改写演化器策略的元演化器；最外层锚点冻结。单谱系设计便于归因。

**关键结果**：报告了边界：超出骨干能力的任务（NLE）上没有提升。

**Summary**: The same frozen model works at three levels: a task harness, an evolver that rewrites it, and a meta-evolver that rewrites the evolver’s strategy; the outermost anchor stays frozen. A single-lineage design aids attribution.

**Key result**: Reports a boundary: no improvement on tasks beyond the backbone’s capabilities (NLE).
