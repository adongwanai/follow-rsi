**Summary**: Formalizes an agent system as a harness–policy pair: failure-guided harness pruning, followed by policy alignment under the surviving harness.

**Key result**: Qwen3-4B/8B exceeds the strongest baseline by up to 12.0% across five benchmarks; pairing within the same round outperforms mismatched pairs.
