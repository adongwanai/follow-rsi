**一句话**：建立在 HGM 之上：把祖先改了什么、哪些行为改善或回退组织成谱系上下文；每个父版本同时生成开放探索子代和针对主要约束违例的专门子代。

**关键结果**：长程规划任务上比最强基线最多高 14.45%（相对）；用 HGM 变体 1/2 到 1/4 的评估预算达到相同最终性能。

**Summary**: Builds on HGM: organizes ancestor edits and behavioral improvements or regressions into lineage context; each parent produces both an open-exploration child and a specialized child targeting the main constraint violation.

**Key result**: On long-horizon planning tasks, exceeds the strongest baseline by up to 14.45% (relative); matches the final performance of HGM variants with 1/2 to 1/4 of their evaluation budget.
