**Summary**: Constructs multiple evidence combinations based on task quality and coverage, generates a candidate harness from each, then eliminates candidates through successive small-budget competitions.

**Key result**: Motivation: AHE peaks at 63.0% with 80 tasks but falls to 57.7% when expanded to 200; SSR’s pass rate keeps increasing as the task pool grows from 40 to 200.
